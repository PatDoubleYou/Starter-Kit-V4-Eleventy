The Problem with Traditional Contracting

Most contractors rely on "free estimates"—vague guesses that leave you exposed to common frustrations. You deserve better.

Budget Overruns

"Allowances" and unforeseen issues often blow up the initial quote, leaving you stressed and over budget.

Schedule Slips

Vague timelines lead to delays, disruptions to your life, and uncertainty about when the job will actually finish.

Poor Communication

Contractors who don't answer calls or provide updates leave you feeling ignored and uncertain about progress.

Our Process is Our Product

We moved the most important part of construction—planning—to the very beginning for clarity and peace of mind.

Step 1: Pre-Construction Service Agreement (PSA)

Instead of a vague "free estimate," our process starts with a paid, collaborative planning phase. We work with you to define the entire project scope, make selections, and get firm bids.

Step 2: Project Success Plan (PSP)

The result of the PSA isn't just a quote—it's your Project Success Plan. This tangible blueprint includes final selections, confirmed costs, a fixed-price agreement, and a realistic schedule. No surprises.

This isn't a trap. Your Project Success Plan is a portable, valuable asset that gives you true budget certainty and peace of mind, whether you ultimately hire us for the construction phase or not.
