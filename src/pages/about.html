Haggerty Construction

A Family Business Built on Trust & Craft

We believe that construction is more than just a job—it’s a craft. Founded by William Haggerty after decades in the industry, our company is built on a foundation of quality, integrity, and a relentless attention to detail. We’re not just building projects; we’re building a reputation with every nail, every cut, and every satisfied client.

As a family-owned and operated business in St. George, our name is on the line. That’s why we approach every project, big or small, with the same commitment: to deliver perdurable work that we’re proud to stand behind.
